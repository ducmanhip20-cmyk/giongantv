Privacy Policy – GIÓ NGÀN TV Poster

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GIÓ NGÀN TV Poster ("the App") is a private tool operated by the owner of the TikTok channel @giongan.tv to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Information we access

With the owner's explicit authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the App accesses only: the basic profile of the authorizing TikTok account, and the ability to upload or publish videos to that same account (scopes requested: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How we use it

The access token is used solely to send videos created by the channel owner to the owner's TikTok account. We do not collect data from other TikTok users, do not sell or share data with third parties, and do not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local configuration file that is not published. They are not transmitted to any third party other than TikTok.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services, after which the App can no longer post.
