GIÓ NGÀN TV Poster

GIÓ NGÀN TV Poster is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the TikTok channel @giongan.tv (GIÓ NGÀN TV, a Vietnamese-language world-news analysis channel).

What it does

The channel owner produces short analysis videos, reviews and approves each video manually, and the tool then uploads the approved video to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. Each upload happens only after the owner's explicit approval. The tool is not offered to other users and does not access any other TikTok account.

TikTok features used

  • Login Kit – the owner authorizes the tool once for the owner's own account.
  • Content Posting API – sends the owner's approved videos to the owner's account.
